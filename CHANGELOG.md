# Changelog

All notable updates to Data Breach: Autonomous Recovery Protocol are documented here.

## v1.0.0 — Portfolio Submission

### Added
- Full 3D recovery mission loop with player movement, enemy AI, objectives, and extraction flow
- Procedural facility environment and interactive world props
- Vi3W-generated player and enemy models integrated into gameplay
- Terminal overlay as the signature WOW feature
- HUD, mission tracking, progression logic, and end-game states
- Architecture, technical notes, and interview-ready documentation
- Targeted bloom, cyan data-core collectibles, and per-instance character material treatments

### Improved
- Tactical camera framing, model grounding/orientation, and floor readability
- Launch responsiveness with an animated protocol sequence and gameplay preloading
- Battlefield visibility by removing overhead blockers and lowering perimeter walls
- Mission clarity and objective visibility
- Visual polish through HUD styling, lighting, and cyberpunk interface design
- Component separation for modular gameplay systems
